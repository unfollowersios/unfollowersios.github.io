Data That Leaves Your Device

The Application works primarily on your device, but some information is transmitted over the internet in order to provide the service. We want you to know exactly what is sent, and to whom:

  • Instagram / Meta: Your login details and app requests are sent to Instagram so that the Application can read your follower and following lists on your behalf. Instagram's own privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Aggregated usage events (such as which screens are opened and whether a purchase or a check-in happened), plus app version, device model, operating system version and language. This is used to understand how the Application is used.
  • Google AdMob: Ad requests, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data. This is used to display and measure ads for users who have not subscribed.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: Your advertising consent choices, where consent is required by law.
  • Trend chart data provider: the public numeric identifier of the Instagram account you are analysing may be sent to a third-party data provider in order to retrieve historical follower counts for the trend charts.
  • Apple: Your purchase receipt, sent to Apple in order to verify your subscription status.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not transmit your Instagram password to any party other than Instagram itself.

Advertising and Tracking

The Application shows advertisements to users who have not subscribed to a premium plan. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob.

On iOS, the Application asks for your permission before allowing the advertising SDK to access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalised advertising. This is the standard App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can:

  • Allow: ads may use your advertising identifier to be more relevant.
  • Ask App Not to Track: ads are still shown, but they are not personalised using your advertising identifier.

You can change this choice at any time in your device Settings, under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where required by law, a consent form is presented before any advertising request is made, and you may review or change your consent choices from the Application's settings.

Subscribing to a premium plan removes advertising from the Application.
